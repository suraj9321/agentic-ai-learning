# Refunds, invoices and billing

## Refund policy

You can request a full refund within **14 days** of your first payment on any paid plan.
After 14 days, monthly plans are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and you keep
access until the end of the billing period you already paid for.

Annual plans cancelled after the first 14 days get a pro-rated refund for the unused full
months, minus the 15% annual discount that was applied.

To request a refund, email billing@nimbus.example from the workspace owner's address and
include your workspace ID (Settings → Workspace → ID). Refunds are returned to the original
payment method within 5–10 business days.

*Policy change: before March 2026 the refund window was 30 days. Purchases made after
15 March 2026 follow the 14-day policy.*

## Double charges and failed payments

If you were charged twice in the same month, this is almost always a pending authorisation
from a failed payment attempt that your bank has not released yet. These disappear within
7 days. If a second charge is still there after 7 days, contact billing and we will refund
it immediately.

When a card payment fails we retry 3 times over 10 days and email the workspace owner each
time. After the third failure the workspace is downgraded to the Free plan. No data is
deleted when downgrading, but recordings beyond the Free plan limits become read-only.

## Invoices and tax

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and are available in Settings → Billing →
Invoices. You can add a VAT or GST number there; it will appear on all future invoices.
Prices are in US dollars. We also accept EUR and GBP on annual plans only.

## Cancelling

Workspace owners can cancel in Settings → Billing → Cancel plan. Your recordings and
transcripts stay available for 90 days after cancelling, then they are permanently deleted.
