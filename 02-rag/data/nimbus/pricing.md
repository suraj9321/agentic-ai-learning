# Pricing and plans

Nimbus is an AI meeting assistant. It joins your calls, records them, writes a transcript,
and produces a summary with action items a few minutes after the meeting ends.

## Plans

**Free.** 1 seat, 5 recorded meetings per month, transcripts are kept for 30 days.
No integrations except Google Calendar. Good for trying Nimbus out.

**Starter: $29 per month.** Includes 3 seats and 100 recorded meetings per month in total.
Adds the Slack and Zoom integrations. Extra seats are not available on Starter; teams that
need more than 3 seats must move to Pro.

**Pro: $99 per month.** Includes 10 seats and unlimited recorded meetings. Extra seats cost
$8 per seat per month. Adds Microsoft Teams (beta), Notion export, custom summary templates
and the public API.

**Enterprise: custom pricing.** Starts at 50 seats. Adds SAML single sign-on (SSO), SCIM user
provisioning, EU data residency, a 99.9% uptime SLA and a dedicated success manager.
Contact sales@nimbus.example to get a quote.

## Annual billing

Paying yearly instead of monthly gives a 15% discount on Starter and Pro. For example, Pro
billed annually costs $99 × 12 × 0.85 = $1,009.80 per year. Annual plans are paid up front.

## AI minutes and overage

Every plan includes a monthly allowance of AI processing minutes (transcription plus
summarisation): Free 150 minutes, Starter 3,000 minutes, Pro 20,000 minutes.
If a workspace goes over its allowance, processing continues and overage is billed at
$0.02 per minute at the end of the month. Workspace owners can set a hard cap in
Settings → Billing → Usage limits to stop processing instead of paying overage.

## Discounts

Registered non-profits and students get 50% off Starter and Pro. Startups in an accelerator
programme can apply for 6 months of Pro for free. Discounts cannot be combined.
