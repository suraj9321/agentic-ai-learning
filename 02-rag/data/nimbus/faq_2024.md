# Frequently asked questions (2024)

**Can I get my money back?**
Yes. We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans, no questions asked.

**How much does Nimbus cost?**
Starter is $19 per month and Pro is $79 per month. Enterprise is custom.

**Do you support Microsoft Teams?**
Not yet. Teams support is on our roadmap for 2025.

**Is my data used to train AI?**
No. Your meetings are never used to train AI models.

**How long are recordings kept?**
Recordings are kept for 60 days on all plans.
