# Getting started with Nimbus

## Create your workspace

Sign up at app.nimbus.example with Google or Microsoft. The first person to sign up becomes
the workspace owner. Owners manage billing, integrations and members. Admins can manage
members and integrations but not billing. Members can only record and view meetings.

## Connect your calendar

Connecting a calendar lets Nimbus join meetings automatically. Without a calendar you can
still invite the bot manually by pasting a meeting link on the home page, or upload a
recording.

## Invite your team

Go to Settings → Members → Invite. You can invite by email or share an invite link that is
valid for 7 days. Anyone with an email address on your company domain can be allowed to join
automatically (Settings → Members → Domain join).

## Your first meeting

Within about 5 minutes of the meeting ending you get an email with the summary, the key
decisions and the action items. Open the meeting page to see the full transcript, search it,
and click any sentence to jump to that moment in the recording.

## Custom summary templates (Pro)

Templates control the structure of summaries: for example a "Sales call" template with
sections for pain points, budget and next steps, or a "1:1" template with wins, blockers
and feedback. Create templates in Settings → Summaries → Templates and assign them to a
meeting series.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- `/` search all meetings
- `j` / `k` next or previous meeting
- `space` play or pause the recording
- `a` add an action item at the current moment
